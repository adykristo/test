8. Saat tes awal, Confirm email boleh OFF. Setelah SMTP benar, aktifkan kembali.

10. Uji end-to-end: daftar -> pending -> pilih paket -> admin aktifkan -> login member -> materi -> latihan -> tryout -> hasil/laporan.
